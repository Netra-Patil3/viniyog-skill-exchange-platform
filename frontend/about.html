OUR STORY

What is Viniyog?

V

Learn • Share • Grow

Viniyog is a student-focused Skill Exchange Platform developed to encourage peer-to-peer learning and knowledge sharing among college students. We believe that every student has something valuable to teach and something new to learn.

Our platform helps students connect with fellow learners, showcase the skills they can teach, discover skills they want to learn, and share useful notes and learning resources. Viniyog aims to make learning more collaborative, accessible, and engaging beyond the classroom.

WHY WE EXIST

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a supportive student community where knowledge is shared freely, skills are developed together, and students help one another grow academically and professionally.

Every student has a skill worth sharing.
